## Conclusions and Retained Limitations

The primary system's overall WER changed from 25.3979% to 9.4828%, a difference of −15.9151 percentage points (95% CI −18.4507 to −13.0477). Long-group WER changed from 27.6288% to 9.3884%, a difference of −18.2403 points (−22.1039 to −14.1928). These two primary outcomes each use unadjusted 95% text-cluster bootstrap intervals, without a 95% simultaneous-coverage guarantee. Overall WER for A→B, the ordinary-training single-factor comparison, was 17.7056% for B. With the same prefix strategy, ANoLM→BNoLM changed from 14.6883% to 9.4828%, providing separate controls for combined and training gains.

B184NoLM's overall WER was 17.0424%. Its medium-length text `alice-p00741-s00-n2` with seed42 reached the 20-second, 500-token cap and produced 213 ASR insertions. Formal statistics retained this attempt. Without human listening, acoustic repetition cannot be distinguished from an ASR decoding loop. This sample did not cross the 30-second window; one B sample and one other B184NoLM sample each lasted 31.68 seconds and crossed Whisper windows. Similar development scores do not establish stability across training seeds. Two training runs cannot estimate the population variance of training randomness.

Length-weighted C was not run. The initial reasonable baseline did not establish stable length-specific degradation, so evidence redirected the project to ordinary training, prefix strategies, and sentence-splitting controls, consistent with the authorized open research route. Sentence splitting changes total token budgets and pauses and is not an equal-compute ablation. Reference-transcript format correction is not counted as training innovation.

Seed183's two OOM events, discarded rollback compute, and independent probes were accounted for; seed184 had no training OOM or restart. The development outer shell's exit127 finalization incident remains recorded, and successful artifacts do not rewrite that exit status. MPS recovery is not bitwise identical; saved paths and mtimes impose recovery constraints. Observed MPS maxima are not continuous peaks. Unknown full-training wall time and energy consumption are not fabricated.

At the time of this audit, the project had run entirely locally, without paid APIs, cloud GPUs, publication, uploads, or coursework submission. The later 2026-10-23 authorization separately permits a curated private GitHub repository. Human listening, MOS, speaker-similarity evaluation, and independent full reproduction in a clean environment were not completed. Under the project requirements, these disclosed evaluation boundaries do not block the automated research deliverable. Gutenberg content can change; exact reproduction depends on retained snapshots. Unknown overlap with upstream pretraining text remains possible.
