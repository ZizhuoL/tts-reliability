# Local Autoregressive TTS Reliability Study: Technical Report

Report status: **local training, development, and the frozen final evaluation are complete**. Each of two training seeds completed one continuation-training epoch. All nine final conditions completed 120 synthesis/ASR attempts each, 1,080 in total, and all 30 stages exited with code 0. The frozen primary system, seed 183/BNoLM, achieved an overall ASR WER of 9.4828% on the independent final set, compared with 25.3979% for the starter. Long and short texts both improved, but seed 184 exhibited a localized heavy-tailed outlier, so stability across training seeds is not established. This report summarizes the implementation and evidence; complete results are in the [final evaluation chapter](final_results.md). Earlier topical documents retain their historical snapshots; their then-pending status does not supersede this completed state.

## 1. Research Question and Observed Findings

The project asks how to generate speech that reliably matches its input text, given a fixed course pretrained model and frozen acoustic components, and how to distinguish gains from continued training, effects of the reference prefix, and changes introduced by sentence splitting. It also requires actual coverage of all valid training data and traceable failures, recovery, timing, and evaluation choices.

The initial motivation was repetition and omission on long inputs in autoregressive TTS. The [CosyVoice 2 report](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.10117v3) describes the relationship between text-to-discrete-speech-token prediction, reference prefixes, and frozen acoustic decoding components. The [NAACL 2025 length-generalization paper](https://aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-long.591.pdf) investigates alignment failures on longer inputs in its encoder-decoder architecture. Its cross-attention alignment method cannot be treated as a directly compatible module for this project's decoder-only checkpoint. The literature motivates hypotheses; it does not replace diagnosis of the course model. See the [research rationale](research_notes.md).

The project first checked natural texts, reasonable length budgets, and the reference transcript. The formal starter baseline's development WER was 26.77%, 22.12%, and 27.52% for short, medium, and long texts, respectively. This did not support a stable, simple overall pattern of “longer is worse.” Accordingly, extreme texts were not concatenated to manufacture length failures, and length-weighted training without supporting mechanism evidence was not presented as a validated innovation. Reference-transcript formatting and LM-reference-prefix sensitivity emerged as more useful factors to control. Local trailing misalignments remain diagnostic candidates; ASR deletions alone do not establish actual spoken omissions.

The final design uses three interpretable factors: course starter versus ordinary-distribution continued training, retaining versus removing the LM reference prefix, and whole-text versus sentence-split generation. The research contribution is the completed and verified chain of evidence. Development supported selection, followed by testing on an independent final set under a protocol frozen in advance; the two stages cannot be pooled into a larger independent test.

## 2. System and Contribution Boundaries

The course provides the architecture specification for an approximately 213-million-parameter causal Transformer, a starter trained for two epochs, and paired LibriTTS text/speech-token data. This project completes the weight-compatible model and data paths and trains that language model on Apple Silicon. CosyVoice2's speech tokenizer, speaker encoder, flow decoder, and vocoder remain frozen, as does Whisper large-v3-turbo used for independent evaluation. The course checkout remains unchanged. This project neither trained the full CosyVoice2 system from scratch nor collected those speech data.

Text is encoded with Qwen2 BPE, and the LM autoregressively generates discrete speech tokens. The frozen flow uses reference-speaker information to produce acoustic representations, and the vocoder produces a waveform. Fixed Whisper transcribes the actual generated waveform, after which text consistency is scored. The main project implementations are target alignment and masking, KV cache, recoverable local training, accounting of actual data exposure, natural-text development/final splits, raw audio and event recording, unified rescoring, and paired analysis. The [architecture guide](architecture_and_interview.md) explains the modules; the [contribution statement](contributions_and_resume.md) distinguishes upstream work from this project's additions.

## 3. Correctness Before Training and Quality Claims

Training uses teacher forcing. For two text tokens and three speech tokens:

```text
Input   [SOS, t0, t1, TASK, s0, s1, s2]
Target  [ -1, -1, -1,   s0, s1, s2, EOS]
```

The TASK position predicts the first speech token, and the last actual speech token predicts EOS. The text prefix and padding do not contribute to loss. The causal mask prevents access to future speech inputs; the padding mask and ignored targets separately control attention and supervision. Full validation is weighted by valid supervised tokens, while each training step uses mean CE over that batch's valid tokens. Matching total supervised tokens and the learning-rate rule therefore does not imply identical stepwise optimization trajectories, attention FLOPs, or runtime.

Implementation checks cover shift/EOS, causality, loss/gradient equivalence under padding, parameter updates, and save/resume behavior. KV cache was checked for stepwise logits, generation, and stopping behavior on CPU and with the actual starter on MPS. It reduces repeated historical computation and is an engineering optimization, not a training-quality innovation. Completed checks and reviews are documented in the [correctness review](code_review.md) and project [README](../README.md). Unchanged tests were not rerun solely to write this report, and small tests are not presented as formal proofs over all inputs.

Recovery saves the model, optimizer, scheduler, sampler, RNG, and cumulative coverage, and verifies source and input identity. Short CPU resume tests were bitwise identical. During actual long-run MPS recovery, data order, counters, and learning rates matched, but numerical values such as loss differed; bitwise MPS reproduction is therefore not claimed. Seed 183 encountered OOM twice at the next batch, 35,592, and rolled back to step 34,000 each time. The final run completed after increasing the MPS memory allowance and saving more frequently. This combined recovery is not a causal ablation of any one memory policy.

The outer development shell ended with exit127 and must not be described as an overall exit0. Completion of the six evaluations, four analyses, and postprocessing was checked separately from that closing error, as recorded in the [execution reconciliation](../results/analysis/development-execution-reconciliation/incident.json). The final runner freezes identity before execution, retains per-stage completion markers, refuses overwrites, and reuses only matching artifacts during resume. These protections still do not replace the integrity checks after the final run completes.

## 4. Two Complete Continuation-Training Endpoints

B183 and B184 **each start from the same starter and independently continue training for one complete epoch**; they are not two consecutive epochs of one model. Both use the ordinary data distribution, the same valid-supervision budget, and the same optimization rules. Each epoch contains 44,348 accepted updates, covers all 354,780 unique valid examples exactly once, and contains 50,450,231 supervised targets including EOS and 58,399,911 valid input tokens. Reconstructed coverage agrees with stepwise logs, and saved endpoint checks match. Seed 184 has no logged rollback; seed 183's discarded branches are accounted for separately. ## 5. Development Design, Paired Results, and Selection

The independent project development set contains 45 natural literary texts: 15 each in short, medium, and long groups, defined by actual text-token counts of 6–17, 18–43, and 44–72. The thresholds come from the training distribution—median 17, p90 43, p99 72—rather than artificially stretched stress-test sentences. The separate final set contains 60 texts, 20 per stratum. Texts come from retained Gutenberg body-text snapshots. Splitting and sampling enforce paragraph separation and deduplication of normalized full sentences, component sentences, and 8-word spans. The checked corpus is the course training/validation text; this cannot guarantee that unknown upstream pretraining never encountered these passages.

The fixed reference is a public LibriSpeech recording with its download revision, transcript, license, and audio hash retained. Formal standard evaluation uses reference metadata with sentence-case capitalization and punctuation but the same word order. No-lm removes the LM's reference text/speech-token prefix while retaining the same acoustic reference. It changes LM conditioning; better WER does not imply unchanged speaker similarity.

The six main development conditions are A/B183 × standard/no-lm/split, each with 45 texts × inference seeds 17/42, totaling 540 terminal records. Pairing fixes texts, reference, sampling settings, and scorer. Each condition has 90/90 scored attempts, with 0 exceptions and 0 context caps. The table gives micro WER; the S/D/I denominator is 2,320 reference words for each system. Complete means pipeline completion only, and EOS does not establish complete reading.

Generation times also varied substantially for identical unsplit audio, so total time alone does not support a causal speedup claim. The only B standard audio exceeding 30 seconds crossed Whisper's chunk window; the corresponding split ASR improvement therefore mixes in an evaluation-path effect. The freeze binds checkpoints, code, configurations, reference, development evidence, and the existing final-set manifest. It uses the final-file hash declared in existing metadata without reading the final text early for freezing; the runner verifies it on first use after the freeze. The protocol's candidate subobject retains its `TEMPLATE_NOT_FROZEN` marker. The outer protocol and its SHA represent the actual frozen status; the candidate alone is not a freeze receipt.

The final plan contains nine conditions, each with 60 texts × 2 inference seeds = 120 attempts, totaling 1,080 planned attempts. The six A/B183 × three-strategy conditions support the planned comparisons; B184 × three strategies provides secondary training-seed sensitivity only. The primary comparison is fixed to A→BNoLM, and the primary outcomes are overall and long-text micro WER. A→B standard is required, explicitly assigned the role of a prespecified research comparison with exploratory interpretation. Other comparisons are exploratory. The final set is not used to reselect methods, seeds, checkpoints, or primary outcomes. Each of the two primary outcomes has its own 95% percentile bootstrap interval without multiple-comparison correction; they do not jointly guarantee 95% coverage, and text-cluster intervals do not provide population-level guarantees across training seeds.

Frozen generation settings include temperature=1, top-k=50, adaptive budget, max_length=500, tokens_per_text=11, min_length_ratio=2, a 0.15-second split pause, and KV cache. LM/flow run on MPS; vocoder/ASR run on CPU. Final artifacts retain raw events, actual WAV files, stop reasons, failures, unified rescoring, text-cluster bootstrap, costs/resources, listening indexes, and source hashes. Automatic exception retries are disabled, and errors cannot silently disappear. Resume and completion rules are documented in the [runner guide](final_evaluation_runner.md).

## 8. Reproduction Scope and Limits of the Conclusions

The project completed locally auditable full-data continued training, development selection, and a frozen independent final evaluation. The preselected seed-183/BNoLM system improved overall, long-text, and short-text performance on the current automatic content-consistency metric. Training, prefix, and splitting contributions are recorded separately through matched-strategy comparisons. The heavy-tailed seed-184 outlier and cross-seed uncertainty also constrain the conclusion; these results do not establish general reliability.

The evidence is limited to English literary texts, one fixed reference speaker, one automatic transcriber, two training seeds, and two inference seeds. No human listening scores, MOS, naturalness measurements, or speaker-similarity measurements were collected. A listening directory selected using actual metrics is not a human evaluation. WER deletion/insertion candidates, normal EOS, and pipeline complete describe different facts and are not interchangeable.

Exact reconstruction depends on retained literary source snapshots, data/model versions, environment locks, and manifests. Online Gutenberg texts may change, and overlap with upstream pretraining cannot be fully excluded. Resume strictly binds input paths/mtimes and source code, so relocating the directory can cause rejection. There is currently no evidence of a complete rerun of the training/evaluation pipeline in a clean environment. Reproduction commands and boundaries are in the [README](../README.md) and [reproduction notes](reproduction_notes.md). Future research should establish a new experimental protocol and evaluation data, without retroactively changing these frozen comparisons or reselecting on this final set while continuing to call it an independent test.
